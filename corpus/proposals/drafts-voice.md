T: ok so imagine you're trying to pour water from a big jug into a tiny bottle. if you just dump it, it splashes everywhere and most of it misses
T: impedance matching is picking the right funnel so the water actually goes in
T: in circuits: if the source and the load "want" different amounts of current, you lose the energy in between as heat or reflection. match them and the power arrives
T: that's the whole thing. the math is just measuring the jug and the bottle
